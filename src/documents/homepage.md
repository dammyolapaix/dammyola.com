## Hero Section

### Headline (Option A - Recommended)

**Build Your Product Without Learning to Code**

_Rationale: Direct benefit. Addresses the core pain point non-technical founders face. Uses customer language ("build your product" not "develop software")._

### Headline (Option B)

**From Idea to Launch in 6-8 Weeks**

_Rationale: Specific timeline creates urgency and sets expectations. Appeals to founders who want speed._

### Headline (Option C)

**You Have the Idea. We Handle the Tech.**

_Rationale: Simple division of labor. Reassures founders they don't need to become technical._

### Subheadline

We build software and AI products for non-technical founders. No technical co-founder needed. No hiring a full team. Just you, your idea, and us.

_Rationale: Expands on headline. Addresses common concerns (no co-founder needed, no full team). Clear positioning._

### Primary CTA Button

**Book a Free Consultation**

_Rationale: Action-oriented. "Free" removes risk. "Consultation" is less intimidating than "call" or "meeting"._

_Rationale: For visitors who want to see pricing/options first. Arrow indicates it's a link, not a button._

## Objection Handling Section (FAQ Preview)

### Section Header

Common Questions

### Question 2

**How long does it take?**

Most projects take 4-8 weeks. Simple features can be done in 2-3 weeks. Full MVPs typically take 6-8 weeks. We work in 2-week sprints so you see progress regularly.

Yes. We work exclusively with non-technical founders. We explain everything in plain English. You don't need to know how to code—you just need to know what you want to build.

Yes. You own everything we build. We'll give you access to the code, help you set up hosting, and show you how to maintain it (or we can maintain it for you).

## Voice & Tone Notes

**Formality:** Professional but friendly  
**Personality:** Helpful, understanding, confident (not arrogant)  
**Language:** Founder-friendly, no technical jargon unless explained  
**Tone:** Conversational, reassuring, direct

**Key Principles Applied:**

- ✅ Clarity over cleverness
- ✅ Benefits over features
- ✅ Specificity over vagueness (timelines, prices, metrics)
- ✅ Customer language ("build your product" not "develop software")
- ✅ One idea per section

---

## Conversion Optimization Notes

**Trust Signals Throughout:**

- Client count: "20+ founders"
- Timeline: "6-8 weeks average"
- Testimonials: Real quotes with names
- Case studies: Specific metrics
- Process: Clear, transparent

**Risk Reversal:**

- "Free consultation" (no commitment)
- "No technical jargon"
- "You own the code"
- "See progress every week"

**Multiple Conversion Paths:**

- Primary: Book consultation
- Secondary: See packages
- Tertiary: Download lead magnet
- Support: Read case studies, FAQs
